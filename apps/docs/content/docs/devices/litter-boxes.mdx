## Safety limits that matter for remote control

These are the limits the manuals set. MyPetKit's own guards for [force continue and auto-pack](/docs/guides/litter-box-automation) are listed separately.

| Limit | Models | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Cats under 6 months: not recommended or prohibited | T3, T4, PURA MAX 2, PUROBOT MAX 3, T5, T5-2, T6, T7 | T3-4 p22, T4 p25, T4-2 p27, T4-3 p27, T5 p38, T5-2 p38, T6 p38, T7 p26 |
| Cats under 1.5 kg (3.3 lb): not recommended or prohibited | T4, PURA MAX 2, PUROBOT MAX 3, T5, T5-2, T6, T7. The T3 manual gives no weight limit. | T4 p25, T4-2 p27, T4-3 p26-27, T5 p38, T5-2 p38, T6 p38, T7 p25-26 |
| Cleaning pauses when a cat approaches or enters, and resumes after it leaves | T3, T4, PURA MAX 2, PUROBOT MAX 3, T6 | T3-4 p20, T4 p9, T4-2 p10, T4-3 p10, T6 p28 |
| "Continuous rotation" ignores that pause and keeps the drum turning | T4, PURA MAX 2, PUROBOT MAX 3 | T4 p16, T4-2 p17, T4-3 p17 |
| Kitten mode turns off automatic and periodic cleaning; "Clean now" still works | T4, PURA MAX 2, PUROBOT MAX 3 | T4 p16, T4-2 p17, T4-3 p17 |
| Litter grains at most 12 mm long and 3 mm across, below the MAX line | T4, PURA MAX 2, PUROBOT MAX 3, T5, T5-2 | T4 p7, T4-2 p7, T4-3 p8, T5 p14, T5-2 p14 |
| Keep cats out while emptying; do not cut power during emptying | T3, T6 | T3-4 p7, T6 p36 |
| If the bin fills while emptying, the box stops (T5) or bags the waste (T6) | T5, T5-2, T6 | T5 p18, T5-2 p18, T6 p27 |

## T3: PURAX (P9901)

- Controls: long press powers on or off; Menu switches function; OK runs it. Functions: clean, spray deodorizer, dump all litter into the waste container, level the litter, turn the drum back to home, connect to Wi-Fi (T3-4 p10).
- Display: network status, today's uses, remaining litter, the cat's weight while it is inside, and fault prompts: lid facing the wrong way, drum knob not locked, paused because a pet came near, check the app for fault detail (T3-4 p11).
- Deodorizer: 50 ml of PETKIT purifying liquid (T3-4 p6). The waste-full sensor misfires if the bag is not smooth and the ring not pressed down (T3-4 p9, p20).
- Power: 12 V, 24 W (T3-4 p21).

## T4: PURA MAX (P9902), PURA MAX 2, PUROBOT MAX 3

- Functions on the panel: clean now, deodorize now, level litter (double-click OK in maintenance), reset (drum home), configure network, maintenance mode (T4 p7-8). PUROBOT MAX 3 lists the same set (T4-3 p9).
- Display prompts on PURA MAX 2: cat approached, cat entered during operation (pauses and alerts), side cover not closed, device failure (check the app), waste bin full (T4-2 p9-10).
- The PURA MAX can bind an "Air Purifier Smart Spray" in the app (T4 p15). Whether that is the K3 is not stated in either manual.
- Consumables on PURA MAX 2: N50 deodorizer about monthly (T4-2 p19). On PUROBOT MAX 3: N50 about 30 days, N60 about 60 days; after replacing N60, tap Reset in the app until it reads "Normal" (T4-3 p16, p19).
- Power: T4 12 V, 24 W (T4 p24); PURA MAX 2 and PUROBOT MAX 3 12 V 2 A, Wi-Fi and BLE (T4-2 p26, T4-3 p26).

## T5: PUROBOT MAX PRO and MAX PRO 2 with Camera (P9904)

- Hardware: fisheye camera, microphone, fill light, camera indicator, OLED, Menu and OK, Breezy odor eliminator, proximity sensor, magnetic side cover (T5 p4-6).
- Panel functions: light, scoop, deodorize, maintenance mode, level litter (double-press OK), empty litter, reset, connect to the network (T5 p27).
- Display icons: network, uses, child lock, camera on, litter low, cat inside, device failure, waste bin full, side cover missing (T5 p28).
- MAX PRO 2 adds two light-assist options, one for toileting and one for cleaning (T5-2 p25).
- Power: 12 V 2 A. MAX PRO 2 adds BLE (T5 p37, T5-2 p37).

## T6: PUROBOT ULTRA with Camera (P9903)

- Hardware: AI camera that you fit with four screws, camera pan, fill light, microphone, speaker, OLED, weight and proximity sensors, a trunk with the auto-bagging module, sealed door, refill ring of bags (T6 p3-6).
- At power-on the drum resets and the camera turns (T6 p9).
- Panel functions: light, scoop, pack, open or close the sealed door, level litter, empty litter, connect to the network, reset (T6 p25).
- Bagging: choose Pack, press OK, then double-press OK when prompted; take the sealed bag out; double-press OK again to load a new bag. The manual says to stay near the device while it packs (T6 p26-27).
- Prompts: bags running out or run out, cat approached, bin not bagged, trunk not fitted, device failure (T6 p28).
- Turning the camera off points it into the box and stops recording; automatic cleaning still runs (T6 p21).
- Consumables: N60 about every two months; refill ring P99034; sifter P99031 (tofu or mixed litter) or P99032 (mineral or bentonite); litter pad P99035 (T6 p3, p6, p9, p35).
- Power: 12 V 4 A (T6 p37).

### T6 troubleshooting table

The T6 manual has the only litter-box troubleshooting table (T6 p36). It uses no codes. It covers four problems: the box does not turn on, the drum does not rotate, bagging fails, and loading a new bag fails.

| Problem | Causes the manual lists |
|---|---|
| Does not turn on | plug or socket, camera not fitted correctly, non-original cable |
| Drum does not rotate | not bound in the app, a pet inside, a part out of place, a broken bag, the bin full, a refill-ring problem, something caught in the drum gear |

The fixes it gives include powering off, refitting the part, and double-pressing OK to resume.

## T7: PUROBOT CRYSTAL DUO with Camera (P9905)

- No screen. Two buttons: Wi-Fi Setup (hold to connect) and Cleaning (start or stop) (T7 p7).
- Lights for Wi-Fi, litter tray, cleaning, camera and microphone; see [Pairing buttons and lights](/docs/devices/pairing-and-indicators#indicator-lights).
- Use only PETKIT crystal litter and trays. Other litter or trays make the product unusable (T7 p26). Discard the tray when its light is solid red (T7 p24).
- AI Lab features: yowling detection and urine pH detection, which reads the colour of pH test particles (T7 p13-14).
- Power: 12 V 2 A (T7 p25).

## AUTOSEAL disposal bin (P9910, P9912)

A battery-powered waste bin with a heat-sealing bag module. The manual mentions no Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or app, and MyPetKit does not support it.

- Hold the button 3 s to power on or off; press it to open or close the sealing door (TP p5, p7).
- Do not seal while the red light is on or while it charges (TP p10, p14).
