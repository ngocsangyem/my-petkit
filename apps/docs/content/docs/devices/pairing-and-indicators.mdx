---
title: Pairing buttons and lights
description: How each model enters pairing mode, what its lights mean, and which Wi-Fi band it needs.
---

All facts on this page come from PetKit's manuals, with the page cited. The manuals describe pairing as part of the PETKIT app flow. With MyPetKit you start the same pairing mode on the device and then use [Add a device](/docs/guides/add-a-device) instead of the app.

## Entering pairing mode

| Model | How | Hold time | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| T3 (PURAX) | Menu to "Configure the network", then OK. The screen shows "Connecting...". | not stated | T3-4 manual, p12-13 |
| T4, PURA MAX 2, PUROBOT MAX 3 | Menu to "Configure network", then OK | not stated | T4 p11, T4-2 p11-12, T4-3 p11-12 |
| T5, T5-2, T6 | Menu to "Connect to the network", then OK. The screen shows "Connecting". | not stated | T5 p12, T5-2 p12, T6 p11 |
| T7 | Press and hold the Wi-Fi Setup button. The Wi-Fi light flashes green quickly while connecting. | not stated | T7 p7-9 |
| D4H, D4H-2, D4-SH, D4SH-2 | In the started-up state, hold the Wi-Fi setup button until the light flashes quickly | 5 s | D4H p10, D4H-2 p12, D4-SH p11, D4SH-2 p11 |
| D4 | Wi-Fi settings button; the manual only says to follow the app | not stated | D4 p1, printed p10 |
| D3-1 | The bowl reset button doubles as the Wi-Fi configuration button | not stated | D3-1 p1, printed p6-7 |
| W7H | Press and hold the Drain button | not stated | W7H p23 |
| CT-W3, EVERSWEET MAX 2 | The app connects over Bluetooth; no button step is given | n/a | CT-W3 p11-13 |

Several manuals say the device does nothing until it is bound: the T5 and T7 must be bound before first use, the T6 runs only after binding, and the W7H dispenses no water until it is paired (T5 p11, T6 p10 and p36, T7 p8, W7H p8).

## Wi-Fi band

| Model | Band | Source |
|---|---|---|
| D4H, D4-SH | 2.4 GHz only. 5 GHz is not supported. | D4H p10, D4-SH p11 |
| D4H-2, D4SH-2 | 2.4 and 5 GHz | D4H-2 p12 and p28, D4SH-2 p11 and p26 |
| W7H | 2.4 and 5 GHz, plus Bluetooth 4.0 | W7H p26 |
| PURA MAX 2, PUROBOT MAX 3, T5-2 | Wi-Fi and BLE, band not stated | T4-2 p26, T4-3 p26, T5-2 p37 |
| T3, T4, T5, T6, T7, D3-1, D4 | Wi-Fi, band not stated | spec table of each manual |
| W4X, CT-W2, CT-W3, EVERSWEET MAX 2, AIRSALON MAX | Bluetooth only | spec table of each manual |
| CT-W1, AUTOSEAL, K3 | No radio stated | |

The T7 manual asks you to place the box near a stable Wi-Fi connection (T7 p5).

## Indicator lights

| Model | Light | Meaning | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| D4 | Wi-Fi | slow blink: offline; fast blink: connecting; on: connected; off: light turned off | D4 p1, printed p11 |
| D3-1 | Wi-Fi | as D4, with fast blink meaning "ready to connect" | D3-1 p1, printed p12 |
| D3-1 | Food level | solid orange: food low; orange and green blinking: malfunction, check the app; blinking orange: low battery; off: enough food | D3-1 p1, printed p12 |
| D4H, D4-SH | The one light | slow white flash: not connected; fast white flash: connecting; solid white: connected, camera not working; solid green: connected, camera working. Not lit in battery mode. | D4H p8, D4-SH p9 |
| D4H-2, D4SH-2 | Wi-Fi | slow white flash: not connected; fast white flash: connecting; solid white: connected | D4H-2 p10, D4SH-2 p9 |
| D4H-2, D4SH-2 | Camera | solid green: on; off: camera off or battery mode | D4H-2 p10, D4SH-2 p9 |
| T6 | Camera | solid green: on; off: camera off | T6 p21 |
| T7 | Wi-Fi | slow green flash: offline; fast green flash: connecting; solid green: connected | T7 p7 |
| T7 | Litter tray | solid green: installed; flashing red: not installed (at start-up: tray facing the wrong way); solid red: replace now | T7 p6-7 |
| T7 | Cleaning | solid green: cleaning; slow green flash: paused | T7 p7 |
| T7 | Camera, microphone | solid green: on; off: off | T7 p7 |
| W7H | Wi-Fi | slow white flash: offline; fast white flash: connecting; solid white: connected | W7H p23 |
| W7H | Water and waste | water shortage: solid red when low, flashing red when empty; waste tank: solid red when full | W7H p24 |

The fountains' lights are on [Fountains and accessories](/docs/devices/fountains-and-accessories).

D3-1's light meanings are matched to their rows by the table's layout in the manual, so treat them with some care.

## Reset and power buttons

No manual describes a factory reset or a way to erase saved Wi-Fi. These are the buttons that do exist:

| Model | Action | Hold | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| D3-1 | Power off for storage: hold the bowl reset and manual dispense buttons together until the voice prompt. It turns on again at the next plug-in. | 5 s | D3-1 p1, printed p18 |
| D3-1 | Restart: press the reset button on the bottom with a pointed object | 10 s | D3-1 p1, printed p18 |
| D3-1 | Zero the bowl scale: press the bowl reset button once | press | D3-1 p1, printed p18 |
| T3, T4, PURA MAX 2, PUROBOT MAX 3, T5-2, T6 | Menu "Reset" turns the drum back to its home position. It does not reset settings. | n/a | T3-4 p10, T4 p8, T4-2 p9, T4-3 p9, T5-2 p27, T6 p25 |
| CT-W1 | Reset the filter timer after a filter change | 5 s | CT-W1 printed p12 |
| K3 | Reset the liquid timer; the light flashes quickly three times | 5 s | K3 front panel 3 |
| CT-W3 | Power the control module on or off | 3 s | CT-W3 p5 |
| AUTOSEAL | Power on or off | 3 s | TP p5 |
