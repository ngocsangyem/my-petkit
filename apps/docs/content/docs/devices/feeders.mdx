## When the network is down

Only the D4 manual describes it (D4 p1, printed p14). The feeder keeps feeding on its schedule. The app cannot reach it, and the records from that time show as "unknown". After it reconnects, at most 10 feeding records are updated, and the schedule cannot be changed during that time. If power fails, it switches to its backup batteries; without batteries it stops until power returns.

## D3-1: FRESH ELEMENT 3 NEW (P560)

- Hardware: manual dispense button, food-level light, Wi-Fi light, bowl reset button (also the Wi-Fi configuration button), reset button underneath, proximity sensors, a stainless bowl on a scale, speaker (D3-1 p1, printed p6-7).
- Built-in 2600 mAh battery and a mains port; 6 V, 6 W (D3-1 p1, printed p19).
- No response after plugging in usually means the battery is empty; wait 10 minutes while it charges. Charge it at least every 6 months if unused (D3-1 p1, printed p18-19).
- Do not put heavy things in the bowl, because it is a scale (D3-1 p1, printed p3-4).

## D4: FRESH ELEMENT SOLO (P570)

- Hardware: Wi-Fi settings button, manual dispense button, Wi-Fi light, lid release, battery compartment for 5 AAA alkaline backups, desiccant compartment (D4 p1, printed p6-7 and p9).
- Power: 6 V DC, 6 W (D4 p1, printed p15).

## D4H: YUMSHARE SOLO with Camera (P571), and SOLO 2 (P572)

- Hardware: indicator light, Wi-Fi setup button, feeding button, camera, microphone, speaker, desiccant compartment, 5 AAA alkaline backups (D4H p4, p6).
- Hold the feeding button for a manual feed. With the camera off, feeding plans keep working (D4H p8, p19).
- The SOLO is 2.4 GHz only; the SOLO 2 also supports 5 GHz and has separate Wi-Fi and camera lights (D4H p10, D4H-2 p10-12).
- The SOLO 2 manual describes matching feeding events to a pet (D4H-2 p14).
- Power: 6 V 2 A (D4H p27, D4H-2 p28).

## D4SH: YUMSHARE DUAL-HOPPER with Camera (P591), and DUAL-HOPPER 2 (P592)

- Two hoppers, 2 L and 3 L, with a feeding button each; a removable partition between them; camera, microphone, speaker; 4 D alkaline batteries as backup (D4-SH p4-5, p7, p28).
- Do not pull the partition out while food is left in the hoppers (D4-SH p8).
- The DUAL-HOPPER is 2.4 GHz only; the DUAL-HOPPER 2 also supports 5 GHz (D4-SH p11, D4SH-2 p11).
- Power: 6 V 2 A (D4-SH p28, D4SH-2 p26).

MyPetKit sends a dual-hopper feeder a separate amount per hopper, because its firmware ignores a single amount. That comes from the firmware, not the manual.

Lights and pairing buttons for all feeders are on [Pairing buttons and lights](/docs/devices/pairing-and-indicators).
