## Bluetooth fountains and the relay range

The CT-W3 manual describes the link MyPetKit relies on for Bluetooth-only fountains. The fountain connects over Bluetooth to a PETKIT litter box or feeder acting as its "master device", within 8 m. That gives remote control and water-shortage alerts. FRESH ELEMENT (P512) and FRESH ELEMENT Mini (P530) cannot be masters (CT-W3 p16). The EVERSWEET MAX 2 manual repeats this (CT-W3-UV-100 p16).

## EVERSWEET SOLO (CT-W1, P4103)

- One key: double-click switches smart and normal mode; hold 5 s to turn the light on or off, or to reset the filter timer after a filter change (CT-W1 printed p5, p12).
- Smart mode runs 3 minutes and stops 3 minutes. It is the default at power-up (CT-W1 printed p11-12).
- Filter every 60 days in smart mode, 30 in normal mode; water every 3 to 4 days (CT-W1 printed p13).
- The English pages mention no app, Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.

## EVERSWEET SOLO 2 (CT-W2, P4114) and EVERSWEET WIRELESS (W4X, P4108)

- Bluetooth, USB powered, wireless pump (CT-W2 p1, printed p5; W4X p1, printed p4).
- Smart mode defaults to 3 minutes on and 3 off, adjustable in the app (CT-W2 p1, printed p4).

## EVERSWEET MAX (CT-W3, P4115) and MAX 2 (P4116)

- Hold the button 3 s to turn the control module on or off; the light flashes blue, green and red in turn. Press it to switch mode while plugged in (CT-W3 p5, p8).
- On battery it cannot switch mode and pumps 25 seconds an hour by default; "Energy management" in the app changes that (CT-W3 p9, p14).
- A pause from the app resumes by itself after 10 minutes (CT-W3 p13).
- Do not rinse the base or the control module (CT-W3 p17).

## EVERSWEET ULTRA AI (W7H, P4117)

- Hardware: camera, microphone, speaker, a 5 L clean-water tank and a 1.8 L waste tank, pump, FlowLift module (W7H p4-5, p26).
- Panel: Water Refill (press to fill automatically; hold to fill, release to stop) and Drain (press to drain; hold for Wi-Fi pairing) (W7H p23).
- Flow modes in the app: continuous, intermittent (3 minutes on and 3 off by default), motion-activated within about 10 cm, and no flow (W7H p13-17).
- Deep clean uses boiling water for about 5 minutes. Keep pets away, and do not stop it part-way: that drains boiling water into the waste tank and can damage the device (W7H p16-17).
- An optional heated tray keeps the water at 20 to 30 °C (W7H p17).
- Power: 12 V 2 A (W7H p26).

MyPetKit removed the W7H's Water Change button because every press rebooted the fountain. That is from testing, not from the manual.

## PURA AIR SMART SPRAY (K3)

- Runs on 4 AA alkaline batteries, 3 to 6 months per set. It starts when batteries go in. Take the batteries out before you change the liquid (K3 front panel 2-3).
- Button: click toggles the light (off again after 2 minutes); double-click sprays; hold 5 s to reset the liquid timer (K3 front panel 3).
- Light: on for 1.5 s at power-up; dim means low battery; a slow 3 s flash after a press means the liquid is low (K3 front panel 3).
- The leaflet does not state the radio. MyPetKit treats the K3 as Bluetooth-only.

## AIRSALON MAX dryer (PD10)

Not supported by MyPetKit. The manual says it ships inactive: the screen flashes until it is bound in the PETKIT app and the in-app safety steps are done (HG p5, p8). It uses Bluetooth, and the phone must stay within 3 m while you change settings (HG p20, p22).
