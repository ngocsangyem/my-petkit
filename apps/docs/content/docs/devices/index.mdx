This section is built from 24 PetKit user manuals and leaflets (the English pages of each, read in full). Every fact here comes from a manual and names the page it is on, written as "T6 manual, p36". Where a manual is silent, these pages say so rather than fill the gap.

For how a model behaves with MyPetKit, see [Supported devices](/docs/supported-devices). This section is about the hardware itself.

## What the manuals do not cover

Read this before you look for anything below.

- **Firmware.** No manual explains how to read, dump, patch or flash firmware. None mentions a serial port, UART, JTAG, a bootloader, SSH, a local API, MQTT, or running without PetKit's cloud. The only firmware instruction, in four manuals (T3-4, D4H, D4-SH, CT-W3), is to keep the firmware updated in the PETKIT app. Everything this site says about firmware comes from the MyPetKit code: see [Firmware and patchers](/docs/firmware).
- **Factory reset.** No manual describes a factory reset or a way to erase saved Wi-Fi. The litter boxes' menu "Reset" only turns the drum back to its home position. The nearest things to a reset are listed in [Pairing buttons and lights](/docs/devices/pairing-and-indicators#reset-and-power-buttons).
- **Error codes.** No manual defines numeric error codes. The litter boxes show text prompts, and "Device failure" says to check the app for detail.
- **Pairing protocol.** The manuals describe pairing only as steps in the PETKIT app. They name no Bluetooth service, access point, port or server.
- **Chips and boards.** No manual names a chipset, flash part or board layout.
