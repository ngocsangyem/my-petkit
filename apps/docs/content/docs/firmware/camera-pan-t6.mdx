---
title: Camera Pan (T6)
description: Pan the Purobot Ultra's camera with the firmware's own factory tool, and what that bypasses.
---

The Purobot Ultra (T6) turns its camera on one horizontal stepper motor. PetKit's app pans it over Agora RTM, a channel that never passes through the cloud MyPetKit replaces, so there is no cloud command to reproduce. The device does ship a factory tool, `/app/bin/pktool`, whose `motor_test` command drives the motor directly. This patcher uses it.

## What you get

**Pan Left**, **Pan Right** and **Pan Home** buttons, in Home Assistant and the dashboard. Each press turns the head about 18 degrees, a tenth of its travel. Left is as seen facing the box. Home turns the camera back into the drum, where the firmware parks it.

## What applying does

Nothing is written to the device and it does not reboot. Applying checks that `pktool` is the exact build the commands were read from (T6 firmware 951) and refuses any other. Every press checks it again, so a firmware update that replaces `pktool` makes the buttons do nothing. Removing the patcher takes the buttons away.

| Command | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `motor_test 2 <steps>` | Relative move. Negative steps turn left. |
| `motor_test 3 <coord>` | Absolute move |
| `motor_test 4` | Status: `coord0=<now>,target0=<goal>` |

Measured on a T6: the travel runs from 0 to 2500 steps. 2500 points into the drum, 1250 is a quarter turn to the left, and one press is 250 steps. Every move landed on the exact step count. The three shell lines the server can send are constants; nothing you type is ever put into one.

## What it bypasses

In normal use `ctrl` owns the motor, with a lock that stops two moves overlapping and a stall monitor. `pktool` goes around both. The kernel driver clamps every target to 0..2500 but has no limit or stall sensing of its own, so a step counter that has drifted (power lost mid-move, for example) can drive the head into its hard stop with nothing to notice.

Three guards narrow that:

- every press runs only if `pktool` is still the verified build;
- every move first reads the status and is skipped unless the motor is idle, so it never races a move `ctrl` is making;
- a pan is refused while the Camera switch is off, so it cannot turn the head out of privacy mode.

If the head ever grinds at an end, stop using the buttons and power-cycle the device so the firmware re-homes it.
